Privacy Policy

This is a demo store. It has no accounts, no server and no analytics, and it never sends what you type anywhere.

To make the store work between visits, your browser keeps a few things on your own device: your bag, Your Saves, the last PIN code you checked and your Light / Dark / Auto choice. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

The checkout form is checked in your browser only. Names, phone numbers, emails and addresses you enter are discarded when the page closes.

Terms of Use

Sales & Refund Policy

If Halo were real: delivery would be free on every order, most items could be returned within 14 days for a full refund, and refunds would go back to the original payment method in 5–7 business days. Engraved items could be returned only if they arrived damaged or faulty.

In this demo, no payment is collected, so there is nothing to refund. Returns & Refunds help ›
